TL;DR

An offer is a discount the salon sets up once that auto-applies to the right clients at booking and checkout — no promo code to type, no math at the desk. Salons can target who gets it (new vs returning clients), what it applies to (services, categories, staff), and when (days, dates, time windows). Pitch it as: "Run smart promotions that fill your slow times — and they just work, automatically."

1. What it is

An offer is a discount promotion created by the salon that automatically reduces the price for eligible clients. The client never types a code — they simply see the discounted price (with the original struck through and a "save X%" style badge) when they book online or at the desk.

Two discount shapes are supported today:

  • Percentage off (up to 99%), and
  • Fixed amount off (down to a $0 floor — it never overcharges or goes negative).
SAY IT SIMPLY

"It's a sale you set up once. The right clients automatically see the right price — no coupon codes to manage."

HONEST SCOPE

Today offers auto-apply based on rules (that's the magic — no codes). Typed coupon/promo codes are coming soon — targeting the next couple of weeks. So pitch "automatic, rules-based discounts" as what's live, and if a prospect wants shareable promo codes, it's fine to tell them that's launching shortly — just don't demo it as live yet.

2. Why it matters (the salon owner's view)

  • Fill the slow times. Tuesday mornings empty? Create an offer that only applies on those days or hours. The discount pulls demand into the gaps you actually want to fill.
  • Win new clients. A "new clients only" offer is a clean acquisition hook that doesn't discount your loyal regulars.
  • Bring regulars back. A "returning clients only" offer re-activates people who haven't been in a while.
  • No front-desk friction. Because it auto-applies, there's no code to remember, no manual discounting, and no arguments — the right price just shows up.
VALUE LINE

"Discounting blindly trains clients to wait for sales. Targeted offers — right client, right service, right day — fill your gaps without giving away margin you didn't need to."

3. How it works

Discount types

  • Percentage off — e.g., 20% off (capped at 99%).
  • Fixed amount off — e.g., $15 off (won't exceed the service price; minimum result is $0).

Who it applies to (eligibility)

  • All clients
  • New clients only — clients with no prior bookings
  • Returning clients only — clients with at least one prior visit

What it applies to (scope)

An offer can target all services, specific services, a service category, or specific staff.

When it applies (targeting the slow times)

This is the powerful part. An offer can be limited by:

  • Days of the week (e.g., Mondays and Tuesdays only),
  • a date window (start/end dates — great for a seasonal promo),
  • a time-of-day window (e.g., 9 AM–12 PM slots), and
  • you can schedule an offer to go live in the future.

Limits and stacking

  • Minimum cart value — only kicks in above a threshold.
  • Per-customer usage limit — cap how many times one client can use it.
  • Total redemptions cap — cap the offer across all clients (or leave unlimited).
  • Stackable vs cart-exclusive — either allow it to combine with other offers, or make it the only discount on the ticket. The system auto-picks the best eligible combination for the client.

Where it shows up

  • Online booking — discounted prices and badges appear on the services page, the date picker, individual time slots, the cart, and the confirm-and-pay screen.
  • Front-desk booking & quick-sale — staff can apply an offer when creating a sale or at checkout on any service line.
  • Staff override — staff can apply an offer in special cases (with a reason), giving the front desk flexibility while keeping a record.

Creating and managing offers

Salons manage offers in Settings → Offers through a short wizard (offer details → scope → eligibility & limits → review & publish). Offers have a clear lifecycle: Draft → Live (or Future if scheduled) → Paused → Expired. Owners can pause/resume, duplicate, edit, and archive offers, and search/filter the list by status.

4. Where it lives + demo path

  1. Create: Settings → Offers → Add offer → walk the wizard: pick a percentage or fixed discount, choose new/returning/all clients, choose the services, and set a day/time window.
  2. See it auto-apply (online): open the public booking page → pick an eligible service/slot → show the struck-through price and the discount badge → carry it through to the confirm screen.
  3. See it at the desk: in Checkout / POS, apply an offer to a service line.
  4. Manage: back in Settings → Offers, show pause/resume, duplicate, and the status filters (Live / Draft / Paused / Expired).
DEMO TIP

The killer demo is a slow-day offer: "I'll make 20% off, new clients only, Tuesdays 9–12." Then show it appear automatically on a Tuesday morning slot and not on a Saturday. That single move shows targeting, automation, and margin control at once. Confirm labels in your demo account first.

6. FAQ & objection handling

"Do my clients have to enter a coupon code?" No — that's the whole point. Offers auto-apply to eligible clients; they just see the lower price. (Typed, shareable promo codes are launching in the next couple of weeks if you want those too.)

"Can I run a discount only on slow days?" Yes — limit an offer to specific days of the week, date ranges, and even times of day. It's purpose-built to fill gaps.

"Can I give new clients a deal without discounting my regulars?" Yes — set eligibility to "new clients only." There's also "returning clients only" to win lapsed clients back.

"What discount types are there?" Percentage off (up to 99%) or a fixed dollar amount off.

"Can I limit how many times it's used?" Yes — per-client limits and a total-redemptions cap, plus a minimum spend if you want.

"Can two offers stack?" You decide per offer — allow stacking, or make an offer the only discount on the ticket. The system applies the best eligible combination.

"Can I set up a promo now to start later?" Yes — schedule an offer to go live in the future, and pause or end it anytime.

"Can my front desk apply a one-off discount?" Yes — staff can apply an offer at checkout (with a reason), so the desk keeps flexibility with a record of what happened.

7. Key terms

  • Offer — a salon-created discount that auto-applies to eligible clients (no code).
  • Percentage / fixed discount — % off (≤99%) or a flat dollar amount off (≥ $0 result).
  • Eligibility — who qualifies: all / new clients / returning clients.
  • Scope — what it applies to: all services / specific services / a category / specific staff.
  • Day / date / time windows — when the offer applies (used to target slow times).
  • Stackable vs cart-exclusive — whether it can combine with other offers or stands alone.
  • Lifecycle — Draft → Live (or Future) → Paused → Expired.
  • Staff override — applying an offer manually at the desk, with a reason recorded.
