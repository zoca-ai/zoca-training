2. Live — but read the caveat

A handful of Scheduling features ship today with a known gotcha.

Booking windows

GOOD TO KNOW

Configurable minimum advance notice and maximum future-booking days — owner sets the window. If a prospect asks "can a customer book six months out?", check their window setting before answering.

Cancellation fees

GOOD TO KNOW

Fee is configurable and only charged when clients cancel within the fee window. Set the window and the fee amount in the policy config — quote both before promising.

No-payment-required policy

GOOD TO KNOW

Default mode when Stripe is not connected. This is the booking-without-payment fallback — useful, but tell the owner they unlock card-on-file, deposits, and tap-to-pay only by connecting Stripe.

7. Key terms (glossary)

  • Booking lifecycle — the state machine a booking moves through: PENDING → BOOKED → COMPLETED / CANCELLED / NO_SHOW.
  • HOLD — a 10-minute reservation on a slot during checkout, auto-promoted to BOOKED on payment success. Prevents two customers racing for the same slot.
  • Policy snapshot — the cancellation / no-show / deposit policy captured at the moment of booking. Tomorrow's policy change does not retroactively apply to today's bookings.
  • Intake form — pre-visit form (allergies, contraindications, etc.). Configurable as once-per-client or every-visit.
  • Approval flow — the three booking-confirmation modes: auto-approve all / auto-approve existing clients only / manual approval.
  • Tap to Pay — in-person card payments via BBPOS WisePOS E or Stripe Reader S700 terminals.
  • Scheduling MCP tools — the agent-facing toolkit that lets Front Desk Conversational AI and Command Centre take real actions on the calendar and the cart.
  • No-payment-required policy — the default booking mode when Stripe is not connected. Lets owners take bookings without entering any payment details.
