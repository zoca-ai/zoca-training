TL;DR

The Brain is one screen with five tabs that together tell the whole story: Canvas (the map of everything your AI knows), Question Bank (questions to answer to get smarter fast), Validate (the inbox of facts to approve), Preview (test what your AI actually says), and Memory History (the full audit trail with one-click undo). In a demo you'll touch four of them in five minutes. Pitch it as: "See it, answer it, approve it, test it, audit it — all in one place."

1. What it is

Open the Brain and you land on a five-tab strip. Each tab is a different job an owner does with their business's memory — and walked in the right order, they tell a complete, reassuring story: here's what we know, here's how it gets smarter, here's how you stay in control, here's the proof it works, and here's the receipt for everything.

SAY IT SIMPLY

"Five tabs, five jobs. One shows you everything your AI knows. One lets you teach it. One is your approval inbox. One lets you test it like a customer. And one is your full history — every change, with an undo button."

2. Why it matters (the salon owner's view)

The five tabs exist because owners need to trust the AI before they'll let it talk to their clients. Each tab removes a specific worry:

  • Canvas answers "what does this thing even know about me?" — at a glance.
  • Question Bank answers "how do I make it useful fast?" — answer a few questions and it jumps ahead.
  • Validate answers "who decides what it can say?" — you do, one card at a time.
  • Preview answers "can I see what it'll actually tell my clients?" — yes, before anyone hears it.
  • Memory History answers "what if it gets something wrong?" — every change is logged and one click reverts it.
VALUE LINE

"By the time you've walked an owner through these five tabs, you've answered the four questions every owner has about AI: what does it know, how do I control it, can I test it, and what if it's wrong."

3. How it works

The five tabs, in the order you'll demo them:

TabWhat it doesWhat to say
CanvasA visual map of every confirmed fact, grouped by topic — Pricing, Cancellation, Staff, Hours, Parking, Add-ons, and more."This is your Brain at a glance — every fact your AI knows about you." (Open first — the wow shot.)

ValidateThe approval inbox. Facts the Brain found — each with a source pill showing where it came from. Four actions: Confirm, Refine, Edit, Reject."We pulled these out of your calls and website — confirm, edit, or skip."

Canvas — the map. Facts cluster by topic, so an owner immediately sees the shape of what their AI knows: a Pricing cluster (e.g., Cut $85, Color $145, Balayage $260), a Cancellation cluster (e.g., $25 fee if under 24 hours), Staff (Maya does color, Jen does cuts), Hours, Parking, Add-ons. It's instantly legible without reading a single line of text.

GOOD TO KNOW

Those prices and fees are the owner's own examples, not Zoca settings. Every business's Canvas looks different — that's the point. Present numbers as "you decide."

Question Bank — the fast lane. Instead of waiting for facts to trickle in, an owner can proactively answer the questions the AI most needs. New accounts get a starter set of questions tuned to their industry on day one, so there's never a blank screen. "Answer ten and your AI gets smarter today."

Validate — the approval inbox. This is the heart of owner control. Every candidate fact shows up here with a source pill — a chip that says where it came from (a call, the website, Google, a chat, a staff save). The owner has four moves on each card:

  • Confirm — yes, that's right; it becomes a usable memory.
  • Refine — mostly right, tighten the wording.
  • Edit — fix the detail, then save.
  • Reject — wrong or irrelevant; it never reaches the AI.

Preview — proof it works. Type a question the way a customer would — "How much is a deep-conditioning add-on?" — and see the exact grounded answer, e.g., "Our deep-conditioning add-on is $40," with a citation showing the Pricing memory it used and where that fact came from. This is the moment an owner realizes the AI is answering from their facts, not guessing.

Memory History — the receipt. Every fact has a full revision log: each version, who confirmed it, the date, and the source. If a fact ever changes wrongly, one click restores the previous version. This is the tab that closes compliance-minded and skeptical buyers.

DEMO TIP

You won't open all five in a demo — that's a trap that drags. The strict path touches Canvas → Validate → Preview → Memory History and skips a deep Question Bank tour. (Full path in Demo & objection handling.)

5. Where it lives + demo path

  1. Log into the dashboard → click Brain in the left navigation.
  2. You land on Canvas — pan around the topic map.
  3. Click Question Bank to show the curated questions (mention it, don't linger).
  4. Click Validate — walk the four actions on a real candidate, then let the owner confirm one.
  5. Click Preview — type a customer-style question and read out the cited answer.
  6. Click Memory History — point at a logged change and the one-click restore.
DEMO TIP

Confirm the Brain is enabled in your demo account (it's off by default) and that the tab labels match what you're saying — labels can vary by version. Use facts that are recognizably "salon" so the Canvas and Preview feel real, not abstract.

7. FAQ & objection handling

"Can I see what the AI is actually telling people?" Yes — that's the Preview tab. Type any question and see the exact grounded answer, with citations to the memory it used.

"What's the difference between the Question Bank and Validate?" Question Bank is where you go answer questions proactively. Validate is the inbox of facts the Brain found and is asking you to approve. One you push to, one comes to you.

"What's that little tag on each card?" The source pill — it tells you where the fact came from (a call, your website, Google, a chat) so you can judge whether to trust it before you confirm.

"What if I confirm something wrong?" Memory History keeps every version. One click restores the previous one. Nothing is ever truly lost.

"Do I have to do anything in all five tabs?" No. Day to day, most owners live in Validate — a quick pass through the inbox. The rest are there when you want them.

"Why does compliance care about Memory History?" Because it's a complete, dated, attributed audit trail of every fact your AI is allowed to say — and it's exportable. That's exactly what legal and compliance teams ask for.

8. Key terms

  • Canvas — the visual topic map of all confirmed facts.
  • Question Bank — the curated list of questions to answer; seeded per industry on day one.
  • Validate / Validation inbox — the tab where candidate facts are confirmed, refined, edited, or rejected.
  • Source pill — the chip on each candidate showing where it came from.
  • Preview — the tab to test the AI's answer with citations.
  • Memory History — the full revision log with one-click restore.
  • Starter questions — the day-one set of industry-tuned questions that seed the Question Bank.
