Discovery is Zoca's digital-marketing agent: it builds the salon's website, writes its blogs and Google posts, optimizes its Google Business Profile, and works the three SEO pillars — local, website, and AI search — 24/7, with no change to the booking platform the salon already uses. It's a 12-month plan and it's the wedge: the easiest, lowest-friction way to start with Zoca, and the on-ramp to everything else. Pitch it as: "One agent that runs your entire marketing, on autopilot."

1. What it is

Discovery is the part of Zoca that does a salon's marketing for them. Instead of the owner juggling a website builder, a blog they never update, a Google listing they forget about, and a vague hope that customers find them — Discovery rolls all of it into one agent that works in the background.

Its own one-line pitch says it best: "Marketing on autopilot. Your website, Google Business Profile, and AI-search presence stay optimized 24/7. No booking platform change required." It introduces itself to a new owner as the agent that "builds your website, writes blogs, gets you found on Google and AI, and creates posts."

SAY IT SIMPLY

"You know all the marketing work you know you should be doing but never have time for — the website, the blogs, the Google posts, replying to reviews? Discovery just does it. Every day. For you."

2. Why it matters (the salon owner's view)

Most small salons are great at the chair and terrible at marketing — not because they don't care, but because marketing is a second full-time job they don't have time for. That creates three expensive gaps Discovery fills:

  • They're invisible where it counts. A stale Google listing, no website or a dead one, and zero presence when someone asks an AI assistant for a salon nearby. Discovery works all three at once.
  • DIY marketing doesn't scale. An owner can write one blog a month if they're disciplined. Discovery puts up a page for every service in the first few days and keeps publishing — far more than any owner could do by hand.
  • They can't see if it's working. Agencies sell a black box. Discovery shows the actual work being done — pages, posts, audits, rankings — on a dashboard.
VALUE LINE

"Every customer who can't find you is revenue walking to your competitor. Discovery's whole job is to make sure that when someone searches — however they search — they find you."

3. How it works

The 3-agent model & why Discovery is the wedge

Zoca sells three agents:

  • Discovery — the digital-marketing agent (this product).
  • Front Desk — the conversational AI that handles calls and messages.
  • Bookings & Payments — the scheduler and register.

They're sold in four combinations: Discovery only · Discovery + Bookings · Discovery + Front Desk · the full suite. Discovery is in every combination — it's mandatory. There is no standalone Front Desk and no standalone Bookings offering today.

That's deliberate. Discovery is the wedge: the lowest-commitment product (no booking-platform switch required), the easiest "yes," and the entry point that leads a customer toward the rest of Zoca. Once a salon's marketing lives on Zoca and is producing results, moving their bookings and payments over is a natural next step.

GOOD TO KNOW

When a customer does take more than one agent, they're onboarded in a fixed order: Discovery → Bookings → Front Desk. Front Desk needs scheduling and availability context to do its job, so Bookings comes before it. You don't need to lead with this on a sales call — but it's good to know if a prospect asks "what's the rollout look like?"

The three SEO pillars

Discovery works on three different kinds of "getting found," and the key insight is that they're genuinely different disciplines:

PillarWhat it meansAspect doc
Local SEOShowing up on Google's local results and the map when someone nearby searches.Local SEO · GBP
Website SEOClassic search ranking on the salon's own website — service pages, blogs, clean structure.Website & content
AI searchBeing recommended and cited inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overview, and Perplexity.AI search (AEO/GEO)

Discovery does all three, with content tailored to each. That "three pillars in one" line is the cleanest way to summarize the product.

UNLEARN THIS

Discovery is not "Google Business Profile management" — and it does not start there. New reps (and most owners) assume discovery = managing the Google listing. Drop that. Discovery means how a business is discovered online — across local SEO, hyperlocal SEO, AEO, and GEO. And the real starting point isn't the GBP at all: it's keyword analysis — what people are actually searching for and the demand behind it. Those keywords are what then drive the Google profile, the website, the content, and the AI presence. GBP is downstream of keywords, not the other way around. If someone told you "Discovery is just the Google profile," unlearn it: Discovery starts from keywords.

The 12-month plan

Discovery is sold as a 12-month plan (annual is the promoted option). Marketing compounds — a website that's been ranking for a year beats one that launched last week — so the year-long commitment is the product, not a billing trick.

Deliverables at scale

This is the part that lands hardest in a demo, because it's something no owner could do alone:

  • Service pages from day zero — one dedicated web page per service. If a salon has 100 services, all 100 service pages go live in the first few days.
  • ~2 blogs/week (configurable) — Q&A-formatted articles with built-in FAQs. The default is around two a week; higher tiers can publish more. (Don't quote a fixed "5/week" — present it as configurable.)
  • Daily Google posts — fresh posts to the Google Business Profile, keeping the listing active.
  • Ongoing optimization — the Google profile and AI-search presence are tuned continuously, not just at setup.
  • Automatic review replies — AI-drafted responses to keep the listing engaged.
VALUE LINE

"This is roughly ten times the marketing output a salon could produce on its own last year — we can optimize twenty services at once instead of four."

7. FAQ & objection handling

"I can write my own content with ChatGPT — why do I need you?"

OBJECTION

You absolutely can write a blog yourself. What you're buying is the productized, at-scale, best-practice version that runs every day without you — a page for every service, blogs and posts published continuously, your Google profile optimized, reviews answered, and all of it personalized to your business so it sounds like you and not generic AI. Doing all those jobs by hand, or hiring an agency to, would cost thousands a month. You're buying the product that does the work, not a chatbot subscription. (See Brain for why the content stays uniquely yours.)

"Can I just buy the booking part?" Not today — there's no standalone Bookings or Front Desk. Discovery is the entry product, and it's part of every package. The good news: Bookings & Payments comes free alongside it.

"Do I have to switch my scheduler?" No. That's the whole point of Discovery as a wedge — it works with whatever you use now. You can move bookings to Zoca later if you want, but you don't have to.

"How is this different from an SEO agency?" An agency hands you a monthly report and a black box. Discovery does roughly ten times the output, shows you the actual work on a dashboard, and covers all three pillars — including AI search, which most agencies aren't even doing yet.
