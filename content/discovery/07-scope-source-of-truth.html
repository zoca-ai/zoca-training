7. Key terms (glossary)

  • Opportunity bucket — High / Medium / Low grouping of services by demand × revenue potential × tracked-keyword count.
  • 5-point geo-heatmap — Map-rank check at five geographic points around the salon per keyword.
  • 7×7 grid scan — On-demand 49-point heatmap for a single keyword. Daily cap applies.
  • Get More Reviews — The automated post-checkout review-request engine. Gated: requires Front Desk + Scheduling.
  • Reserve with Google — Booking feed upload that puts a "Book" button on the GBP listing.
  • RCA (Root-Cause Analysis) — Automated diagnosis of why a location is underperforming.
  • Content Queue — Discovery's content scheduler and publisher. Lives under Discovery; cannot run standalone.
  • Supervised mode (Content Queue) — Optional approval step (Needs Approval → Approved / Rejected) before publish. Default mode is auto-publish.
  • AEO / GEO — Answer Engine Optimization / Generative Engine Optimization. The AI Search product. Currently entitlement-gated.
