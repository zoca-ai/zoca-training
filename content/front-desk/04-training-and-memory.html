TL;DR

The owner shapes Zoe in the Training tab: Business Profile and Services are required to go live; QnAs, Policies, and Custom Instructions fine-tune her. The owner picks one of four voices (and can rename her). Zoe gets smarter through a question-and-answer loop with the Brain — calls surface things she couldn't answer, the owner answers, and that becomes knowledge. The flagship upcoming feature is Configs (e.g. letting Zoe offer an on-the-spot discount). Pitch it as: "She sounds like your salon, not a robot."

1. What it is

Zoe isn't a generic bot — the owner teaches her about their specific business. That happens in the Training tab, where the owner fills in who they are, what they offer, their policies, and any special instructions. They also choose Zoe's voice and can give her a name. Over time, Zoe gets sharper as the owner answers the questions real callers ask.

SAY IT SIMPLY

"You spend about half an hour teaching Zoe your business — your services, your prices, your policies, the questions people always ask — and from then on she answers like someone who works there."

2. Why it matters (the salon owner's view)

A receptionist who doesn't know the business is worse than no receptionist. The Training tab is how Zoe avoids that — and the question loop is how she keeps getting better without the owner doing extra work.

  • She knows the menu and the prices. Because Services sync from the connected scheduler, Zoe quotes real numbers, never a guess.
  • She answers the FAQs. Parking, hours, "do you do balayage," "is there a cancellation fee" — the owner loads these once.
  • She gets better over time. When a caller asks something Zoe didn't know, that question is surfaced for the owner to answer — so the gap closes itself.
VALUE LINE

"The first week, she knows what you told her. By the second week, she knows the things your customers actually ask — because every call she couldn't fully answer becomes a question you answer once, and she's got it forever."

3. How it works

The Training tab has a top auto-section ("Questions from calls") plus five owner-configured sections:

SectionWhat it holds
Business ProfileCore business facts. Required to go live.
ServicesThe service catalog, synced from the booking platform. Required to go live.
QnAsQuestion-and-answer pairs Zoe can use.
PoliciesFree-text policies (e.g. cancellation) — a name/type plus the body.
Custom InstructionsSpecial instructions injected into how Zoe behaves (the platform's own behavior wins on conflict).
GOOD TO KNOW

The onboarding team pre-fills Business Profile and Services from the salon's website, Google listing, and a 30-minute discovery call — the owner just reviews and tweaks. Edits take effect on the next call after saving, so there's no waiting.

OBJECTION

"This sounds like a lot of setup." — It's not on you. Our team pre-fills the heavy parts from your website and a quick discovery call; you review. From there, Zoe surfaces the questions she couldn't answer and you knock them out one at a time. The work shrinks over time, it doesn't grow.

The "Questions from calls" loop (the Brain). When a caller asks something Zoe couldn't fully answer, that question gets surfaced at the top of the Training tab (with a Brain badge). The owner answers it, and that answer becomes permanent knowledge.

GOOD TO KNOW

Be precise here: Zoe does not secretly mine call transcripts and invent memories. She surfaces questions she couldn't answer; the owner's answer is what becomes knowledge. That's a trust feature — the owner stays in control of what Zoe "knows."

4. Voice & persona

The owner picks one voice for their salon from four options, in Agent Settings:

VoiceCharacter
ErynWarm & professional
VexaExpressive & energetic
MarkCasual & relaxed
JessicaFeminine & kind
  • The default name is "Zoe," but the owner can rename her to anything (front desk's name, the owner's name, a brand mascot).
  • The voice and the name are independent — you can name her "Frances" but she'll speak in whichever of the four voices you chose.
  • There's no voice cloning — Zoe can't be made to sound like a specific person. One of the four, picked per salon.
  • The voice is fixed for a given call; changing it applies to the next call.
DEMO TIP

If the prospect is a high-energy, trendy salon, demo with Vexa; a calm spa, with Eryn or Jessica; a barbershop that wants a guy's voice, with Mark. Matching the voice to the brand makes the "she sounds like us" point land.

GOOD TO KNOW

SMS style: when Zoe texts, she's concise, no markdown, emojis used sparingly. She can receive picture messages; sending them is limited.

In-call memoryWhat's said during this one call (so she doesn't ask the same thing twice).This call only.
Caller identity + booking historyRecognizing a returning caller by phone and seeing their upcoming appointments.Ongoing, via phone match.
Brain knowledgeThe business facts, FAQs, and answered questions the owner has taught her.Indefinite.
ROADMAP

Be honest about what memory does not do yet: Zoe can't recall what a client got last visit, doesn't carry preferences across calls ("always book me with Sarah"), and doesn't read or write client notes (allergies, VIP). All on the roadmap. Today she recognizes you and sees your upcoming appointments — not your full history.

6. Configs — the flagship upcoming feature

ROADMAP

Configs (~1–2 months · flagship). Configs will let the owner set rules so Zoe "acts more like the owner would" — the headline example being on-the-spot discounting: letting Zoe offer, say, 20% off to convert a hesitant caller. This is the big upcoming customization capability. It's a great reason to buy now, but do not demo it as live — it isn't yet.

GOOD TO KNOW

Related roadmap items in this area: an Offers / Promotions section (Zoca Scheduling only, ~2–3 weeks) and Front-Desk-branded confirmations/reminders (~3–4 weeks; today the connected platform sends those). Per-stylist hours and date overrides aren't confirmed in the Training tab yet — the workaround is a Custom Instruction like "closed July 4."

7. Where it lives + demo path

  1. Dashboard → Front Desk → Training.
  2. Show the five sections; point out Business Profile and Services as the go-live requirements.
  3. Open Services and note it's synced from the connected scheduler — "these are your real services and prices."
  4. Show the top "Questions from calls" section and explain the answer-once loop.
  5. Open Agent Settings and switch between the four voices so they can hear the difference; show the name field.
DEMO TIP

Playing two different voices back to back ("here's Eryn, here's Mark") is a small wow moment — it makes "she sounds like your salon" tangible. Confirm labels in your own account first.

8. FAQ & objection handling

"Can I make her sound like me / a specific person?" Not in v1 — there's no voice cloning. You pick one of four voices (warm, energetic, casual, kind). You can name her whatever you like, though.

"How does she learn my prices?" Your services sync from your scheduler, so she quotes your real menu — no manual typing of every price, and no made-up numbers.

"Does she eavesdrop on calls and decide things on her own?" No. When she hits a question she can't answer, she surfaces it for you; your answer becomes her knowledge. You stay in control of what she knows.

"Can she remember that I always see Sarah / what I got last time?" Not yet — that's on the roadmap. Today she recognizes you by your number and sees your upcoming appointments.

"Can I have her offer a discount to close someone?" That's the flagship feature coming in a month or two — Configs. I won't demo it as live because it isn't yet, but it's exactly where we're headed.

"How fast do my changes take effect?" Next call after you save. No waiting period.

GOOD TO KNOW

There's a known v1 wiring issue with QnAs, Policies, and Custom Instructions fully reaching the live agent — fixes are in progress and it's treated as a v1 fix (not a roadmap feature). If a prospect's edits don't seem to land, that's the cause; flag it to the product team rather than promising a timeline.

9. Key terms

  • Training tab — where the owner teaches Zoe (Business Profile, Services, QnAs, Policies, Custom Instructions).
  • Business Profile / Services — the two required-to-go-live sections.
  • QnAs — question/answer pairs Zoe can use.
  • Custom Instructions — special behavior rules for Zoe (platform behavior wins on conflict).
  • Questions from calls — the auto-surfaced list of things Zoe couldn't answer, for the owner to answer.
  • Brain — Zoca's shared knowledge store that Zoe feeds via the answer-once loop.
  • Voice — one of four (Eryn / Vexa / Mark / Jessica), chosen per salon; no cloning.
  • Configs — the flagship upcoming feature (e.g. on-the-spot discounts).
