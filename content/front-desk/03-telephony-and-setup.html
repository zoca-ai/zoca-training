1. What it is

Zoe answers on a dedicated number that Zoca sets up and pays for — a real US/Canada phone number that handles both calls and texts. The salon doesn't have to give up its current number: they forward their existing line to Zoe's number, so customers keep calling the number they already know.

Setup is a guided, ~45-minute onboarding (rarely over an hour) that ends with a free test call and a "go live" step.

SAY IT SIMPLY

"We give you a fresh number that Zoe answers on — we pay for it. Then we forward your real number to it, so your customers dial the exact same number they always have, and Zoe picks up."

2. Why it matters (the salon owner's view)

Two fears kill phone-tool deals: "Will I lose my number?" and "Is this a painful tech project?" Both are handled.

  • The number stays. Customers, Google listings, business cards, the sign on the door — nothing has to change. Forwarding does the work invisibly.
  • Setup is short and guided. It's about 45 minutes with help, not a multi-day IT project.
  • You stay reachable. Zoe transfers callers to a human during the hours the owner sets, so a client who needs a person still gets one.
VALUE LINE

"You don't lose your number, you don't reprint anything, and you're live the same day. The phone just starts getting answered."

3. How it works

The dedicated number. Zoca provisions a US/Canada number for calls and SMS (no fax). The owner can choose the area code so it feels local. Zoca also handles the carrier trust registration that lets the number send texts reliably — that part takes a few days during onboarding, in the background.

Keeping the existing number. Two live options:

  • Call forwarding — the salon's carrier forwards calls to Zoe's number. A guided wizard handles this.
  • GBP swap — swap the number shown on the Google Business Profile to Zoe's number.
ROADMAP

A true self-serve number port-in (moving the actual number onto Zoca) is on the roadmap. Today a real port is ops-assisted — don't promise instant self-serve porting. Forwarding is the standard path and works now.

The forwarding wizard. This is the single most-touched support surface, and it's friendly: a few steps, support for 28+ carriers, with a universal fallback (dial *72 to turn forwarding on, *73 to turn it off) if a carrier isn't listed. The owner picks how calls forward — there are nine modes:

GOOD TO KNOW

"After hours" and "No answer" are the gentle starter modes — Zoe only catches the calls the salon would've missed anyway. Owners nervous about AI usually start there and move to "All calls" once they trust her.

Transfer / handoff. Zoe can transfer a live caller to a human during the salon's transfer hours. Today that's a cold transfer to one destination per salon. Outside transfer hours, Zoe says the owner is unavailable and takes a message.

ROADMAP

Warm transfer (Zoe briefs the human before handing over) and multiple transfer destinations are on the roadmap. Today: cold transfer, one destination.

Call recording & consent. Every call is recorded by default for quality, silently. Be aware of two-party-consent states (including CA, FL, IL, MD, MA, MT, NH, PA, WA, among others) where both parties must be informed recording is happening. The salon owner is responsible for the disclosure, not Zoe. Zoe does NOT auto-announce recording at the start of calls — owners in two-party-consent states should set up their own notice (an outgoing voicemail message, an on-hold notice, or signage at the desk for in-person follow-ups). Recording retention is governed by our voice provider.

COMPLIANCE NOTE

Zoe doesn't auto-announce call recording — the owner does. Auto-announce is out of scope for the product. In states that require disclosure (two-party-consent), the owner sets up their own notice. When you're demoing to a prospect in one of those states, be explicit: the disclosure belongs to them, not to the agent.

Spam shield. On by default. Known junk numbers can be short-circuited, very short calls get flagged, and obvious robocalls are classified out after the call so they don't clutter the owner's view. (It's post-call filtering of the obvious stuff, not a perfect pre-answer wall — see What Zoe does.)

7. FAQ & objection handling

"Do I lose my number?" No. You keep it — your customers dial the same number, and it forwards to Zoe behind the scenes. (Actually moving the number onto us is ops-assisted and on the roadmap, but you don't need that — forwarding works today.)

"Who pays for the phone number?" Zoca provides and pays for it. You can pick the area code so it feels local.

"I don't want AI answering every single call." You're in control of when. Start with "after hours" or "no answer" so Zoe only catches calls you'd miss anyway, then expand once you trust her.

"Can it put me on the line when a client needs me?" Yes — Zoe transfers to one destination during the hours you set. (Warm transfer and multiple destinations are coming; today it's a direct transfer to one number.)

"Are calls recorded?" Yes, by default, for quality. Heads-up if you're in a two-party-consent state (like California) — both parties need to be aware recording's happening. Zoe doesn't auto-announce that on the call — you set up the disclosure (outgoing voicemail note, on-hold message, or desk signage for follow-ups). We can point you at options, but the notice is yours to put in place.

"How long does setup take?" About 45 minutes, rarely over an hour, with us helping the whole way — connect your calendar, pick a voice, set up forwarding, fill in your basics, do a free test call, go live.

8. Key terms

  • Dedicated number — the US/Canada number Zoca provides (and pays for) that Zoe answers on.
  • Forwarding — sending the salon's existing calls to Zoe's number; the standard way to keep the old number.
  • GBP swap — changing the number on the Google Business Profile to Zoe's.
  • Port-in — actually moving the number onto Zoca (ops-assisted today; self-serve is roadmap).
  • Forwarding mode — one of nine rules for which calls go to Zoe (all, after-hours, no-answer, etc.).
  • Transfer / handoff — Zoe passing a live call to a human during set hours (cold transfer, one destination today).
  • Two-party-consent state — a state where both parties must be told a call is recorded.
  • Go-live — the final onboarding step; requires a connected scheduler + Business Profile + Services.
