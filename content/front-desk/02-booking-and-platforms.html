This is the core win. Most AI phone tools just text the caller a booking link or only work with Calendly. Zoe actually writes the appointment into the scheduler the salon already runs on — Booksy, Vagaro, Fresha, Square, GlossGenius, Mindbody, or Zoca's own — end-to-end, live on the call. A connected platform is required (there's no booking-less mode), and Zoe books without collecting a deposit or card so most bookings land confirmed. Pitch it as: "She writes the booking into YOUR calendar — not a link, not a maybe."

1. What it is

When a caller wants an appointment, Zoe doesn't hand them off to "click the link in the text I just sent." She does the booking herself, on the call: she looks up the salon's real services, checks real availability on the real calendar, and creates the appointment in the salon's existing scheduler. By the time the caller hangs up, the appointment is on the books.

The same is true for rescheduling and cancelling — Zoe can move or cancel an existing appointment in the connected scheduler too.

SAY IT SIMPLY

"Whatever calendar you use today — Booksy, Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius, Fresha — Zoe books straight into it. You don't switch tools, you don't learn a new system. She just works inside the one you've got."

2. Why it matters (the salon owner's view)

The owner's #1 fear with an AI phone tool is: "Is it going to create a parallel mess I have to reconcile?" The answer is no — Zoe writes into the one calendar the salon already trusts.

  • No second calendar to babysit. The appointment lands where the owner already looks every morning.
  • No "click the link" drop-off. Every step you add between "I want to book" and "I'm booked" loses people. Zoe closes the loop on the call, so a hot lead doesn't cool off staring at a text.
  • No tool migration. The salon keeps Booksy or Vagaro or whatever they love. Zoe rides on top of it.
VALUE LINE

"The other tools send a link and hope. Zoe books. The difference between 'here's a link' and 'you're booked for Saturday at 2' is the difference between a maybe and a client."

3. How it works

Under the hood, Zoe speaks to each scheduler through a dedicated connector, but the caller experience is identical no matter which platform the salon uses. On the call she can:

  • Look up services — the real menu, synced from the platform, with real prices.
  • Check availability — the real open slots for the right stylist.
  • Book — create the appointment in the scheduler.
  • Reschedule / cancel — modify an existing appointment.
  • Find the right stylist — match the caller's request to the right provider.
GOOD TO KNOW

A connected booking platform is required. There's no "info-only, no-booking" mode — Zoe is built to book, so the salon has to be on one of the supported schedulers (or Zoca's own). If a prospect isn't on any scheduler at all, the move is to put them on Zoca Scheduling.

5. The deposits-off policy

Zoe books without collecting a deposit or taking a card on the call. This is deliberate, and it's a feature, not a gap.

  • The salon's own deposit settings in their scheduler stay exactly as they are — Zoe just doesn't enforce them when she books.
  • Because there's no deposit step to stall on, most Zoe bookings land confirmed instead of stuck in "awaiting payment."
  • It keeps the call short and frictionless, which means more callers actually finish booking.
VALUE LINE

"Your deposits stay, your revenue grows. We don't touch your deposit settings — Zoe just gets more people fully booked instead of dropping off at a payment screen."

ESCALATE

If a salon insists Zoe must enforce their deposit / collect a card on the call — do not promise it and do not improvise. Escalate on the call to Ashish, Robin, Chintan, Kushagra, or Piyush. The toggles for deposit enforcement exist but are intentionally off; building it on is a product decision, not an AE one.

8. FAQ & objection handling

"Do I have to switch to your calendar?" No. Zoe books into whatever you already use — Booksy, Vagaro, Fresha, Square, GlossGenius, Mindbody. You keep your tool. (If you're not on any scheduler, that's when Zoca Scheduling comes in.)

"I'm on Boulevard." That one's on the roadmap — we don't have a connection for it yet, so I won't promise it works today. Let me confirm timing for you.

"What if Zoe books over an existing appointment / double-books?" She checks real availability on your real calendar before booking, so she's working off the same open slots you'd see. Be honest that she's very good, not perfect — but she's reading your live calendar, not guessing.

"Why doesn't she take a deposit? I rely on deposits." Your deposit settings stay untouched — Zoe just doesn't collect on the call, which gets more people fully booked instead of stalling at payment. If you genuinely need her to enforce deposits, tell me and I'll get the right person on it. (Then escalate per §5.)

"Does Vagaro cost extra?" Sometimes — Vagaro may need a paid-tier upgrade, around $10/month more on their side. Worth surfacing up front so there are no surprises.

"Can she cancel and reschedule too, or only book?" All three — book, reschedule, and cancel, right in your connected scheduler.
